Our business idea

Why CampusBrew?

We want to fill the gap between very cheap machine coffee and more expensive cafés outside school.

₱

Student-friendly price

Our plan targets ₱20 per cup, based on the survey finding that most respondents preferred coffee below ₱20.

⚡

Made for short breaks

A small, simple menu and a campus-focused location aim to make coffee easier to get between classes.

☕

Better coffee choice

CampusBrew aims to offer a better-quality alternative to basic vending-machine coffee without café-level prices.
